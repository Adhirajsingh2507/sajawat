# SAJAWAT BRAND GUIDELINES

## PURPOSE

This document defines the visual identity, design language, and brand standards for Sajawat Jewellery.

All UI, UX, marketing, content, and product presentation must follow these guidelines.

---

# BRAND POSITIONING

Sajawat is a:

Luxury Jewellery Brand

Not:

* Budget Brand
* Discount Brand
* Generic Marketplace

The website should feel:

* Premium
* Elegant
* Royal
* Modern
* Trustworthy
* Aspirational

---

# BRAND PERSONALITY

Primary:

* Luxury
* Elegant
* Royal
* Premium

Secondary:

* Traditional
* Modern
* Bold

Every design decision should reinforce these traits.

---

# LOGO USAGE

Primary Logo:

Purple + Gold Sajawat Logo

Rules:

* Always maintain clear spacing.
* Never stretch logo.
* Never distort logo.
* Never rotate logo.
* Never apply random colors.

Preferred Backgrounds:

* White
* Deep Purple
* Very Light Gold

---

# COLOR SYSTEM

## Primary Color

Royal Purple

Usage:

* Header
* Hero Sections
* Premium Highlights
* CTA Sections

---

## Accent Color

Luxury Gold

Usage:

* Buttons
* Premium Labels
* Icons
* Borders
* Highlights

---

## Neutral Colors

White

Off White

Light Gray

Dark Gray

Black

Used for:

* Backgrounds
* Text
* Supporting UI

---

# DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Target Experience:

"Luxury jewellery showroom"

Not:

"Generic e-commerce website"

Users should immediately feel:

* Premium
* Trust
* Quality

---

# LAYOUT PRINCIPLES

Use:

* Large spacing
* Clean layouts
* Strong visual hierarchy
* High-quality imagery

Avoid:

* Clutter
* Crowded layouts
* Excessive text blocks

---

# TYPOGRAPHY

## Headings

Luxury Serif

Characteristics:

* Elegant
* Premium
* High-end

Examples:

Playfair Display
Cormorant Garamond

---

## Body Text

Modern Sans Serif

Examples:

Inter
Poppins

Characteristics:

* Readable
* Clean
* Modern

---

# BUTTONS

Primary Button:

Royal Purple Background

Gold Accent

Rounded Corners

Strong Hover State

---

Secondary Button:

White Background

Purple Border

Purple Text

---

Never use:

* Neon Colors
* Bright Red CTAs
* Generic Bootstrap Styling

---

# PRODUCT CARDS

Must Include:

* Product Image
* Product Name
* Price
* Wishlist Icon

Optional:

* Best Seller Badge
* New Arrival Badge

Design Goals:

* Clean
* Premium
* Focus on product imagery

---

# PRODUCT IMAGERY

Images are the most important sales asset.

Requirements:

* High Resolution
* Consistent Lighting
* Consistent Background

Avoid:

* Blurry Images
* Watermarked Images
* Inconsistent Product Photography

---

# ANIMATION GUIDELINES

Animation Level:

Medium

Use:

* Fade In
* Slide In
* Hover Effects
* Smooth Transitions

Avoid:

* Excessive Motion
* Distracting Effects
* Slow Animations

Animations should support luxury, not entertainment.

---

# ICONOGRAPHY

Style:

Modern Minimal

Prefer:

* Lucide
* Heroicons

Avoid:

* Cartoon Icons
* Overly Decorative Icons

---

# TRUST ELEMENTS

Include:

* Reviews
* Contact Information
* Social Media Links
* Secure Checkout Indicators

Trust should be visible throughout the customer journey.

---

# HOMEPAGE STYLE

Hero Focus:

Offer Banner

Followed By:

* Categories
* Collections
* Best Sellers
* New Arrivals
* Reviews
* Blog
* B2B Section

Goal:

Encourage exploration and purchase.

---

# MOBILE DESIGN

Mobile First.

Requirements:

* Thumb-Friendly Navigation
* Fast Loading
* Optimized Images
* Minimal Friction

Mobile experience must never be treated as secondary.

---

# CONTENT TONE

Use:

* Elegant
* Professional
* Warm

Avoid:

* Aggressive Sales Language
* Cheap Promotional Language
* Excessive Capitalization

---

# DO NOT

Do Not:

* Copy competitors directly
* Use generic templates without customization
* Overcrowd pages
* Use inconsistent colors
* Sacrifice luxury feel for short-term trends

---

# SUCCESS TEST

Every page should pass this question:

"If a customer visits this page for 5 seconds, does it feel like a premium jewellery brand?"

If the answer is no, redesign the page.
